Hi, I’m Yoonsoo. I am a PhD Candidate at the University of Wisconsin–Madison School of Nursing (advisor: Kristine Kwekkeboom, PhD, RN, FAAN). My research focuses on symptom experiences and recovery after critical illness.

I am interested in developing and testing patient- and family-centered symptom self-management interventions incorporating technology, including digital health and AI. My dissertation examines post-ICU symptom experiences in ICU survivors through the I SEE Study.

Before joining UW–Madison, I earned my BSN and MSN at Seoul National University and worked as a medical ICU nurse at Seoul National University Hospital.

Outside of research, I enjoy singing and do busking from time to time, and running.
